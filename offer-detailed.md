## 6. Risk reversal / guarantee ⚠️ **NEEDS RECONCILIATION + LEGAL REVIEW**

**Problem to solve first:** the existing sites contradict themselves — one place promises **50% of your money back if 50% isn't deleted**, other pages promise **100%**. Four pages, two different guarantees. This must become **one** clear statement before any of it appears in an ad.

**Compliance caution:** a guarantee tied to *deletions* is exactly the outcome-guarantee that draws CROA/FTC scrutiny. Safer risk-reversal framings to consider (for legal review):
- **"The strategy call is free — if you don't have a case, you'll leave the call knowing that, at no cost."** (Naturally true, safe, and lowers the barrier to the actual conversion event.)
- A **service/satisfaction** guarantee rather than an outcome/deletion guarantee.
- Any performance-based structure must be reconciled with CROA's **no-advance-fee** rule (see Open items).

**Do not publish a specific money-back percentage until Vance picks one and a CROA-competent attorney signs off.**

## 9. Urgency & scarcity (honest levers only)

- **Genuine urgency (use):** harassment is happening *now*; every day of inaction is another day of calls and another day the inaccurate item damages their score/approvals; some FCRA/FDCPA claims are **time-sensitive** ⚠️ (confirm framing)
