CTA + Urgency Swipe File

Grab-and-go button copy, urgency, scarcity, and the reassurance microcopy that rides next to every button. Click any line to copy it. Swap the [brackets] for your offer.

RULE 01

Start with a verb

"Get", "Start", "Claim" — never "Submit" or "Buy now".

RULE 02

Name what happens next

The button promises the result, not the mechanic.

RULE 03

First person lifts clicks

"Get my access" often beats "Get your access". Test it.

RULE 04

Keep it true

Fake timers & counters kill trust — and break the law in some places.
