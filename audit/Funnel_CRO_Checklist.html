Scoring: Priority = (Impact + (4 − Effort)) × urgency, where urgency is 1.33 while a checkpoint is "To do" and 1.0 once "To improve" (max ≈ 8). Done / N/A score zero and drop out of the queue. Defaults are a sensible starting point — tune per client.
