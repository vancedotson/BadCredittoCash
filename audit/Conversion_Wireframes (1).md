# Conversion-Optimized Page Wireframes — Info Product Funnel

A build-ready blueprint derived from the E-commerce CRO Checklist, adapted for a **digital / info product** and a **merged sales + order page**. Each page is a top-to-bottom wireframe with **what it is**, **why it matters**, and **example copy**.

> How to read it: blocks stacked vertically = scroll order. `[ ]` at the end of each page = your build/QA list.

**Pages**
1. Sales + Order Page (merged, info product) ✅
2. Thank You Page _(coming next)_
3. Upsell Page _(coming next)_

> **Physical → Info translation (applies throughout):** no shipping address, courier, delivery date, stock counts, size charts, variants, or photo gallery. Instead: instant/lifetime access, a sales video + curriculum preview, money-back guarantee, and time/cohort-based scarcity. Email is your delivery channel — treat it as critical.

# 1 · Sales + Order Page (merged)

> One long page. The top 90% persuades; the bottom 10% is a focused, link-free order form. A sticky CTA scroll-jumps buyers to the form from anywhere. Golden rule: **sell the transformation, then make buying frictionless and exit-free.**

## Section-by-section

### A. Sticky bar
- **What:** Product name, price, ★rating, and one CTA — **"Get instant access"** — that scroll-jumps to the order form. Hides on scroll-down, returns on scroll-up.
- **Why:** On a long page the form is far away; this keeps the buy action one tap away at any scroll depth.

### B. No exits
- Logo shown but **not clickable**; no top nav, no footer links leading off-page. The only forward motion is toward the form.

### C. Hero (above the fold)
- **Headline:** the core transformation, a power word, under ~65 characters. *"Write code that lands the job — in 6 weeks."*
- **Subhead:** who it's for + the promise.
- **Sales video (VSL):** replaces the physical photo gallery. This is your single biggest persuasion asset for an info product — autoplay muted or click-to-play, with a clear ▶.
- **Rating overview:** `★4.8 — 1,200 students`, links down to testimonials.
- **Benefit bullets:** 3–5 green-check outcomes.
- **Primary CTA:** scrolls to the order form; copy says what happens: *"Get instant access — $149."*
- Price (localized), money-back guarantee line, express-pay logos right here.

### D. Trust strip
- "As seen in" / PR logos, student count, headline result. `1,200 students · featured in X · avg. result Y`.

### E. Problem → Agitate → Solution
- Info products sell a *transformation*, so narrative replaces a spec sheet. Name the pain, deepen it, then position the product as the bridge.

### F. What's inside (curriculum)
- The info-product equivalent of the product description. **Benefit-led module titles** (not "Module 3" but "Module 3 — Land interviews on autopilot"). Long lists in **accordions** so mobile stays scannable. Show total lessons/hours, downloadable resources, and any community/access perks.

### G. How it works — in 3 steps
- *1. Enrol → 2. Follow the weekly path → 3. Ship your result.* Removes "what am I actually buying / how do I use it" friction.

### H. Social proof
- Testimonials with **face photo, full name, "verified," occupation**, and a specific result. Video testimonials. Screenshots of student wins / messages. Filterable ratings. These convert skeptics far more than star averages.

### I. Meet the creator
- Founder story, credentials, mission/vision. For info products the buyer is partly buying *you* — this section does heavy lifting.

### J. Value stack + bonuses
- List each component with a $ value, then the single price beneath it. *"Core course $199 + Templates $99 + Live Q&As $149 = $447 value. Today: $149."* Add relevant bonuses (the digital equivalent of cross-sell).

### K. Pricing / offer
- If you have tiers, show them side by side with badges: `Basic` · `Pro — Recommended` · `Bundle — Best value`. Anchor with the highest tier.

### L. FAQ (objection handling)
- Cover product-specific *and* purchase questions: access duration, refund policy, device compatibility, "do I need experience," payment options.

### M. The order form (locked-down zone)
This is the only checkout — keep it tight and exit-free. Digital means **fewer fields than physical**:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Email (first)** | Your delivery channel + abandon-recovery. Capture before anything else. |
| Name | For the receipt/account. |
| Billing country | Only if you need it for VAT/tax — otherwise skip. |
| **No shipping address** | Nothing ships. Don't ask for it. |
| Payment | Radio buttons (Card / PayPal / Apple Pay) on a **gray panel**; CVV hint icon; card inputs sized to format. |

Form behaviour (same rules as physical): single column, floating labels, input suggestions, inline green/red validation, errors that explain the fix and never wipe the form, field persistence, correct mobile keyboards (numeric for card, @/.com for email), guest checkout with optional login autofill.

### N. Order summary (beside the form)
- Price with strike-through + **"You save $50."**
- Coupon hidden behind a small "Have a code?" link.
- **Order bump** (digital): a one-tap, low-cost add-on — *"+$27: Add the Templates Pack"* — no payment re-entry.
- Total near the CTA, instant-access reassurance, guarantee, express pay, installments (if higher-ticket).

### O. The CTA + final reassurance
- Most prominent element. Copy: **"Complete order — get instant access"** with a 🔒 and a distinct background. Trust seal + "Shop with confidence" directly below.
- Restate the guarantee one last time after the form, with a final CTA for readers who needed the full scroll.

## Scarcity & urgency (info-product flavour)
- Time: `Price goes up Friday at midnight`.
- Cohort: `Only 50 seats in this cohort`.
- Bonus deadline: `Free live Q&A access ends Sunday`.
- Live activity: `38 people enrolled this week`.
- Avoid fake "3 left in stock" — there's no stock; it reads as dishonest for digital.

- [ ] Hero leads with a VSL, not a photo gallery
- [ ] Headline <65 chars, benefit + power word; rating links to testimonials
- [ ] Problem→agitate→solution narrative present
- [ ] Curriculum with benefit-led module titles in accordions
- [ ] 3-step "how it works"
- [ ] Testimonials with faces, names, verified, results; video proof
- [ ] Creator story / mission section
- [ ] Value stack with $ anchoring + bonuses
- [ ] Pricing tiers with "Best value" badge (if applicable)
- [ ] FAQ handles refund, access, compatibility, experience-level
- [ ] Order form: email first, NO shipping address, minimal fields
- [ ] Payment on gray panel, CVV hint, correct mobile keyboards
- [ ] Inline validation; errors explain fix; form persists
- [ ] Summary: savings, hidden coupon, total, instant-access note
